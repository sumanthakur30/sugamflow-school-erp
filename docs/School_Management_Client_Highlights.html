SugamFlow School ERP

School Management Application

Key highlights for client sharing — configurable, secure, and integrated school operations

1. One Unified Platform

Manage the complete student journey—from admission and enrollment to attendance, fees, examinations, promotion, and transfer certificates—in one system.

2. Key Capabilities

  • Online admission with configurable approval workflows
  • Automatic student enrollment and admission-number generation
  • Student 360° profile (guardians, academics, attendance, fees, library)
  • Global student and staff directories with search and export
  • Class, section, subject, teacher assignment, and timetable
  • Daily attendance with teacher roster and parent alerts
  • Examination, gradebook, report cards, and PDF generation
  • Fee structures, concessions, collections, and receipts
  • Library catalog, issue/return, fines, and clearance
  • Hostel bed inventory, allocation, and release
  • Transport routes, vehicles, stops, and student assignments
  • Payroll workflows and operational previews
  • Parent and teacher self-service portals
  • School announcements via Communication Hub
  • Bulk student import with validation, dry-run, and commit
  • Homework/LMS foundation with future external LMS options
  • Promotion, academic lifecycle, and transfer certificates

3. Designed for Each Stakeholder

Management: dashboards, approvals, reports, audit trails, and multi-campus visibility
Administrators: admissions, records, fees, operations, imports, and configuration
Teachers: attendance, gradebook, report cards, and academic workflows
Parents: attendance, examination results, report cards, and notifications
Students: academic information and future homework/LMS services

4. Business Benefits

  • Reduces repetitive administrative work
  • Creates a single, reliable source of school data
  • Improves fee and operational transparency
  • Provides faster communication with parents
  • Minimizes errors through validations and approval workflows
  • Supports data-driven school management
  • Improves accountability with complete audit history

5. Flexible and Configurable

  • Feature-based subscription plans
  • Configurable forms, workflows, rules, menus, and notifications
  • Multi-school, multi-campus, and academic-session support
  • Custom branding, localization, and module settings
  • Role-based access with tenant and branch-level data isolation

6. Reliable and Secure

  • Role-based permissions and restricted stakeholder access
  • Tenant isolation between schools
  • Audit logs and configuration rollback
  • Health monitoring and operational-readiness checks
  • Responsive interface for desktop, tablet, and mobile
  • Offline synchronization support for selected activities
